Converted images

Abhi koi image nahi.
Converted images yahan dikhengi. Kisi bhi image pe click karein to original aur WebP ka muqabla slider se dekh sakte hain.

Kaise kaam karta hai: pehle image ko poori resolution pe sab se oonchi quality par try kiya jata hai; agar size zyada ho to quality ko binary search se itna adjust kiya jata hai ke limit mein fit ho. Agar "kam se kam quality" par bhi fit na ho, tab hi resolution thori kam ki jati hai — is tarah tasveer dhundli ya blocky nahi hoti. Bohat bari photos (jaise 12 MP phone camera) ke liye 1920 px aur 100 KB ek acha balance hai. Safari mein WebP encoding support nahi — Chrome, Edge ya Firefox istemal karein.
